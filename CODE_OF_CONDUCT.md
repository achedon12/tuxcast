# Code of conduct

tuxcast follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient, especially with people new to Linux. Streamers come
here because something broke right before going live; answer the question they asked, not
the distribution they chose. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated, in
issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub's private contact](https://github.com/achedon12) or by email to the address on the
maintainer's profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments,
lock threads and ban participants who break this code, as the Covenant describes.
